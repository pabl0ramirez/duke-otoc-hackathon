# Receipts for the BlueQubit Midwest Quantum Hackathon, 21 to 22 November 2026

The second hackathon after Duke (the rest of this repository). Same kind of task, out-of-time-order correlators
on hidden circuits of up to 38 qubits, run only through BlueQubit's classical simulators, each problem scored as
the squared correlation with the organizers' exact values.

## Placement

18th on the final leaderboard, 2762/3000, 16 submissions of ours, last submission 22 November 2026 12:00 PM as displayed, as shown in our
screenshot of [our row](leaderboard_our_row.png) (sha256 b5204ad86dc0d519...), taken by us on 23 November 2026 after
the organizers released the raw scores. We could not find a public copy of the leaderboard, so this screenshot is our
receipt for the placement.

## Why the total is higher than the live scores below

During the event the leaderboard showed each problem's score rounded down to the nearest 10 percent. The organizers
announced on the event's Discord on 23 November 2026 that this was done "to avoid attempts at probing and reconstructing our solutions from
the scores", and that the raw scores they then released are all strictly higher. Our live scores add up to 2620; the
raw total is 2762.
